How it works

Three steps from sign-up to application.

  1. Create your account

    Sign up with your university email address, programme and student number.

  2. Complete your profile

    Add your skills, interests and CV. Your university coordinator verifies your profile.

  3. Apply and track

    Apply to the opportunities that suit you and follow each application's status.
